About this app

Time Tracker — On Purpose is a free, open-source app for tracking billable client work and personal time. It's dependency-light and hosted on GitHub Pages, with no company server involved.

Your data & privacy

Everything you enter is stored only on this device until you sign in with Microsoft, above. Once signed in, it's saved to a private app folder in your own OneDrive — nobody else, including Rachel, can see or access it. If you never sign in, your data stays local to this device and browser only, and is gone if you clear your browser data.

Open source

The code is free for anyone to view, use, or build their own copy from, under the MIT license. View it on GitHub.
