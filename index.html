Zones

Zones are your time-tracking categories — one per project, or type of work. Each gets its own timer, colour, and total in Reports.

References

A reference is an extra layer of detail you can add to your time tracking log (for example, client initials, staff names, or project names). It also appears in Reports, so you can group or filter by it there.

About this app

Time Tracker | On Purpose is a free, open-source app for tracking work across multiple priorities, for work or personal time. There's no company server behind it. Your device talks directly to Microsoft on your own behalf, so nobody sits in between you and your own data. It was designed and directed by Rachel Mackay, and built in collaboration with Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant.

Your data stays private

Everything you enter is stored only on this device until you sign in with a Microsoft work account, above. Once you sign in, it's saved to a private app folder in your own OneDrive, and nobody else, including Rachel, can see or access it. If you never sign in, your data stays local to this device and browser only, and it's gone if you clear your browser data.

Add it to your home screen

On your phone, open this page in your browser and add it to your home screen (iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open the browser menu, then Add to Home Screen or Install app). On a computer, most browsers let you install it from the address bar, or you can just save the page to your favourites.
